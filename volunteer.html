Volunteer with Youth Connect

We welcome committed individuals who believe in our principles, value service-oriented work and want to contribute their time and skills without expectation of remuneration.

Volunteer Principles & Conditions

✓ Agree with the principles and ideals of Youth Connect Foundation.
✓ Have a genuine interest in service-oriented and community-focused work.
✓ Volunteer without expectation of salary, allowance or other remuneration.
✓ Maintain honesty, accountability and trustworthiness in project implementation.
